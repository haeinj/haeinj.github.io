Boiling Physics

Making boiling predictable for high-heat-flux thermal management.

The Scientific Challenge

Boiling enables efficient heat removal through phase change, yet its complex interfacial dynamics make heat transfer and thermal failure difficult to predict across operating conditions. Empirical correlations remain useful within their validated ranges, but their transferability is often limited.

Research Goal

My research aims to uncover the governing mechanisms of boiling and develop physically consistent predictions of heat transfer and the onset of boiling crisis. I use artificial intelligence in conjunction with physical understanding and experimental evidence to investigate these phenomena and evaluate predictive models.

Practical Impact

The long-term goal is to enable more effective and reliable high-heat-flux cooling by improving our ability to predict boiling performance and thermal limits.
